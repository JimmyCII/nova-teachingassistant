# Legitimate Digital & Online Access Options: enVision Mathematics 2021 Common Core Student Edition, Grade 6 (Savvas)

## TL;DR
- The only fully authorized way to read the complete Grade 6 student edition online is through **Savvas Realize** (savvasrealize.com) via a paid license/subscription or a bundled purchase; there is **no free, public, full-text digital copy** anywhere legitimate — not on Google Books, Amazon, public libraries, OverDrive/Libby, or the Internet Archive.
- For an individual/parent, the cheapest legitimate digital path is the **enVision Math 2021 Homeschool Bundle, Grade 6 ($156.00, ISBN 9798213022568)** including 1-year Realize access; institutional digital-courseware licenses start at **$31.00/year per student**; educators can get a **free 30-day Realize demo**.
- Free, legal *previews* exist only as **registered "virtual samples"** on Savvas.com plus **third-party aligned content** (IXL, EdReports, Mathleaks); the Internet Archive and OverDrive carry only **older, different editions**, and the IA copy is locked to print-disabled patrons.

## Key Findings

1. **Savvas Realize is the official platform.** It is a cloud-based HTML5 LMS at https://savvasrealize.com hosting the interactive student eText plus all digital courseware. Students/teachers log in with a username/password issued by a district/school Savvas administrator, or via single sign-on (EasyBridge, Clever, ClassLink, Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology). There is no consumer "buy one eText" button — access is provisioned through a license.

2. **There IS a free trial for educators.** Savvas's demo page states verbatim: *"Please fill out the form to demo enVision Mathematics for Grades K-12 on Savvas Realize, free for 30 days"* (savvas.com/demo-request/math/envision-k12-demo). A separate "free resources" sign-up offers *"three weeks of FREE K-8 math resources"* — but those samples are drawn from the newer **2024** edition, not the 2021 edition.

3. **License pricing (2021 edition, Grade 6)** is published on the Louisiana state adoption price list (Savvas contract #4400030866). Standalone digital courseware and student-edition+digital subscription tiers run from **$31.00/year** up to **$165.00 for a 6-year bundle** per student. Print student-edition volumes are **$16.50 each**.

4. **Public preview/table of contents on official channels is gated.** Savvas offers grade-level "virtual samples" but only after registering a request. Google Books shows only bibliographic "About" pages with **no eBook available**, and Amazon shows catalog listings without a reliable full "Look Inside."

5. **Public libraries do not carry the 2021 edition.** Neither OverDrive/Libby nor Sora list the 2021 enVision Mathematics Grade 6. The Internet Archive has only an **older 2015 edition**, restricted to print-disabled patrons (not publicly borrowable).

6. **No school district publishes the full digital edition openly**, and there is no official open PDF/flipbook on Savvas; districts gate it behind student logins.

## Details

### 1. The official publisher platform: Savvas Realize
Savvas Realize (https://savvasrealize.com) is Savvas Learning Company's cloud-based learning platform and the sole authorized host of the complete interactive Grade 6 student eText along with the program's digital courseware (Visual Learning Animations, Desmos tools, MathXL practice, videos, assessments, editable resources). It is built in HTML5 and works on modern browsers, iPads (7th gen+), Android 10+ tablets, and Chromebooks.

**How students/teachers access it:** All users log in with one set of credentials, either directly at savvasrealize.com or through a district portal. Districts typically roster users through Savvas EasyBridge (Basic/Auto/Plus) and enable single sign-on via Clever, ClassLink, Google Classroom, Canvas, or Schoology. Individual students cannot self-enroll into the licensed course; an administrator/teacher provisions the account. Password resets are handled at the sign-in screen via the "Forgot your username or password?" link.

**Free trial/preview:** Yes, for educators — Savvas offers a **free 30-day demo** of enVision Mathematics K-12 on Realize through a demo-request form (savvas.com/demo-request/math/envision-k12-demo). A separate "enVision Free Resources" form (savvas.com/resource-request/envision-free-resources) gives about three weeks of downloadable sample lessons, but those samples are explicitly from the **2024** edition ("three weeks of FREE K-8 math resources from our updated enVision® Mathematics © 2024 program").

**License cost:** Realize access is sold as a per-student license/subscription, generally to schools/districts, and to homeschoolers via bundles. See pricing below.

### 3. Public previews, sample pages, and table of contents
- **Official Savvas virtual sampling:** Savvas provides grade-level component samples for enVision Mathematics 6-8 at savvas.com/solutions/mathematics/core-programs/envision-mathematics-grades-6-8/data/pages/virtual-sampling, but you must register/request access; it is not an open, anonymous preview.
- **Google Books:** Only bibliographic "About" records exist with "No eBook available" — no readable preview of the 2021 student edition.
- **Amazon:** Catalog/product listings for Grade 6 Volume 1 (ISBN 9781418269470) and Volume 2 (9781418269487), published by Scott Foresman, dated Nov 1, 2019. No reliable full "Look Inside" of content.
- **Third-party but legitimate:** **EdReports** gave enVision Mathematics Common Core Grades 6-8 © 2021 its highest "All-Green"/"Meets Expectations" rating across all three gateways (Focus & Coherence, Rigor & Mathematical Practices, Usability) — per Savvas's Dec 3, 2020 announcement that the program "received the highest rating from EdReports.org." The free EdReports Grade 6 report confirms the program devotes **approximately 81% of instructional time to the major clusters of the grade** and lists the topic structure. **IXL** publishes a free skill plan aligned to "enVision Mathematics 2021 Common Core Grade 6" (aligned practice, not the textbook). **Mathleaks** and **Lumos Learning** offer aligned solutions/practice keyed to the program's topics.
- The publicly visible topic structure — e.g., **Topic 1 "Use Positive Rational Numbers"** (confirmed verbatim in EdReports' Grade 6 report), Topic 2 "Integers and Rational Numbers," Topic 5 ratios/rates/unit rates — appears via third-party teacher materials and aligned resources, providing an effective table of contents even without an official open preview.

### 4. School-district open publishing
No school district publishes the complete 2021 Grade 6 digital student edition openly. Districts that have adopted enVision (e.g., East Brunswick Public Schools, NJ) describe the program publicly and explain how students access it, but the actual eText sits behind student Realize/SSO logins. Some district pages publish login instructions (e.g., student-ID-based credentials), but those still require an enrolled, licensed account. This means there is no lawful "open" district copy to read.

### 5. PDF preview / flipbook on official site or authorized partners
There is no official open PDF or flipbook of the 2021 Grade 6 student edition on Savvas.com. The eText is delivered as a secured, accessible ePub/PDF only inside Realize Reader (it supports offline use, read-aloud, translation, and is WCAG 2.0 AA / Section 508 compliant). Savvas also uploads NIMAS files to the NIMAC for authorized accessible-format use by qualifying students with print disabilities. Various unofficial PDF/flipbook copies circulate on file-sharing sites; those are unauthorized and are excluded here.

### 6. Other legal/authorized access routes
- **Library lending (not available for this edition):** The Internet Archive's only relevant item is an older, *different* edition — "enVision Math Common Core Realize Edition, Grade 6" (Randall Charles, ISBN 9780328808113, dated 2015), and it is **locked to print-disabled patrons** ("Access-restricted-item: true," collection_set "printdisabled," "No suitable files to display here," no Borrow button). A second IA item ("enVision Math 6, Virginia Edition," 2012, ISBN 9780328611010) is identically locked. Neither is the 2021 book and neither is publicly borrowable. **OverDrive/Libby** list only legacy elementary "enVisionMATH Common Core" titles (e.g., G1, G5) from Pearson Learning Solutions — not the 2021 Grade 6 — and their real-world availability depends on a specific library having purchased a copy. **Sora** is restricted to K-12 institutions and is not available to individuals or homeschoolers.
- **Accessibility (NIMAC):** Students with qualifying print disabilities can obtain authorized accessible formats through their school via the NIMAC.
- **Spanish edition:** A Spanish 2021 Grade 6 edition exists with its own subscription/license ISBNs (e.g., 1-yr subscription + digital courseware 9780768582697, $52.00) if needed.

## Recommendations
1. **Parent/independent learner wanting legitimate digital access now:** Buy the **enVision Math 2021 Homeschool Bundle, Grade 6 (ISBN 9798213022568, $156.00)** from homeschool.savvas.com or an authorized reseller. This is the only consumer-facing way to legally obtain the full interactive eText on Realize (1 year), and it includes the print workbooks. Locate the Homeschool Digital Access Card on arrival and follow its activation steps — digital access is not active until you register it.
2. **Educator/curriculum reviewer evaluating content:** Request the **free 30-day Realize demo** (savvas.com/demo-request/math/envision-k12-demo) and register for **virtual samples** on the enVision 6-8 page. Read the free **EdReports "All-Green" review** for an independent quality analysis before committing budget.
3. **School/district:** Purchase a **Digital Courseware license** (1-, 3-, or 6-year; from $31.00/student/year) or a student-edition-plus-digital subscription, and roster students through EasyBridge/SSO (Clever, ClassLink, Google Classroom). Use state-contract price lists as a budgeting benchmark.
4. **Cost-constrained, need only practice/alignment:** Use **IXL's free enVision 2021 Grade 6 skill plan**, **EdReports**, and the program's published topic list as a no-cost supplement — but recognize these are not the textbook itself.
5. **Do not rely on the Internet Archive, OverDrive/Libby, or public libraries** for this specific 2021 edition — it is not legitimately available there.

**Benchmarks that would change these recommendations:** If Savvas releases an open flipbook/PDF preview of the 2021 edition, or if a public library/OverDrive adds the 2021 Grade 6 title, the free-preview and library routes would become viable. For a single year, the 1-year digital license ($31.00) or student-edition+digital subscription ($41.50) beats the homeschool bundle; for multiple children or multiple years, the 6-year bundle ($165.00) is far more cost-effective per year.

## Caveats
- Prices cited are from a state adoption contract (Louisiana, contract #4400030866, posted Oct 11, 2024) and from Savvas's homeschool storefront; they are benchmarks. Retail/reseller prices and availability differ by state and vendor and may have changed.
- The 2021 "enVision Mathematics" program is distinct from the older "enVision Math 2.0" (2017) and "enVisionMATH Common Core" (~2011-2015) programs. Library, Google Books, and used-book listings frequently conflate them, so verify ISBNs (Vol. 1 = 9781418269470; Vol. 2 = 9781418269487; 6-year subscription bundle = 9780768582291; homeschool bundle = 9798213022568).
- Homeschool digital access is geofenced to the 50 U.S. states and conditioned on non-institutional use.
- Unauthorized full-text copies exist on file-sharing sites; these fall outside legitimate access and are deliberately not recommended or linked here.
